# IconButton

Round icon-only button. The onboarding back button is the `light` variant: 40px, `surface-2` fill, black arrow.

- **Consumer provides:** `icon`, `label` (required — becomes `aria-label`), `variant` (`light` | `surface` | `ghost` | `glass`), `size` (default 40).
- `glass` only over photos and the camera; `ghost` for overflow and close inside cards.
