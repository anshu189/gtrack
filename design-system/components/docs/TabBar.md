# TabBar

Main navigation. `variant="bar"` is the Figma home: a full-width white bar with three tabs (Home, Progress, Settings) and the 64px FAB floating over its top-right. `variant="pill"` is the floating four-tab pill from the marketing screens.

- **Consumer provides:** `variant`, `value` + `onChange` (or `defaultValue`), optional `items`, `onAdd`.
- Active tab: black label and filled icon; inactive `ink-2`.
