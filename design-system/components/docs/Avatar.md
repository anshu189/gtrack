# Avatar

Round user avatar: photo, or initials on the blue gradient (`avatar-2` → `avatar`).

- **Consumer provides:** `initials` or `src` + `alt`, `size`, optional `streak` (count badge under the avatar) and `crown` (leader ring in `streak`).
- White initials on `avatar` are 2.46:1 — kept from the source; prefer photos, or larger initials.
